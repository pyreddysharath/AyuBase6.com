Privacy Policy

AyuBase6 website (ayubase6.com) and AyuBase6 Android app · Last updated 04-Nov-2026

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-up.
  • What you enter (your choices, routines, reminders, readings and lab values) stays on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Ads are shown only after you agree to Advertising. The ad provider may then use cookies or your device's advertising ID.
  • If you send feedback, we receive the name and message you type.

Data you enter stays on your device

Body and Food choices, your wake-up time and Checks, reminder settings and My Reminders, Vitals readings and lab values are saved only in the website's storage in your browser, or in the Android app's own storage on your phone. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Lab report PDFs you open are read on your device to find the values; the file is not uploaded.

Backup saves your data to a file that you keep (for example in Downloads). Restore reads such a file back. We never see these files.

Clearing the browser's site data, clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app deletes this data. Make a Backup first if you want to keep it.

Reminders and notifications

Reminders are worked out and shown on your device. In the Android app they are scheduled with the phone's own alarms, so they also arrive when the app is closed. The app asks for permission to show notifications, and for "Alarms & reminders" so they arrive at the exact minute. Nothing about your reminders is sent to a server.

Content loaded from the internet

The website and the app load their videos, the lists of videos, announcements and the app's version number from ayubase6.com (hosted on GitHub Pages). Some features use common libraries from public content networks (for example the PDF reader). Like any website, these servers receive your device's IP address and basic request details in order to send the files. Videos you save for offline use are stored on your device.

Advertising

AyuBase6 shows ads from Google AdSense, on the website and in the Android app (through Google's Mobile Ads SDK for web views), only after you agree to Advertising in the cookie settings. You can change this at any time under Settings → Cookies.

When ads are on, Google and its partners may use cookies, your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (from your IP address), device type and how you interact with the ads, to show ads, measure them and limit how often you see the same ad. See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).

Feedback

If you use Help → Feedback, the name and message you type are sent to us (stored in a Google Sheet through Google Apps Script) so we can read and reply. Please do not include sensitive personal or medical details. To have your feedback removed, send a feedback message asking for it.

Health information

AyuBase6 gives general guidance, not medical advice, and is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before changing your exercise, diet or medicines.

Children

AyuBase6 is meant for adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. For questions, use Help → Feedback on ayubase6.com or in the app.
