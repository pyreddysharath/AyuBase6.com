## Full description
AyuBase6 brings the basics of a healthy day together in one simple app.

BODY
• Exercise and yoga plans for your age group, with short videos
• Save the videos once and play them without internet

FOOD
• A nutrient and food plan for your age, gender, BMI and food preference (vegetarian, non-vegetarian or vegan)
• A weekly shopping cart, recipes and a food plan you can download as a PDF

CHECKS
• A gentle daily routine built around your wake-up time: water, sunlight, movement, meals and sleep
• Reminders at the start of each activity, and before its time window ends
• Tick an activity straight from the notification with ✓ Done, or tap Not yet to be asked again in 15 minutes

REMINDERS
• Eyes (20-20-20), Walk, Water and a guided Eye Exercise
• Your own reminders, at set times or every few minutes
• Choose your reminder hours and the days they ring
• Reminders arrive on time even when the app is closed, the phone is locked or has been restarted
• Turn a reminder off straight from its notification

MIND
• Short, practical thoughts for the morning, the day and the evening

VITALS
• BMI, and lab values such as blood sugar (HbA1c, glucose), cholesterol and thyroid, with what they mean
• Read values from a lab report PDF, and track your readings over time

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
• No account and no sign-up
• Everything you enter is kept on your device
• Back up your data to a file and restore it on a new phone

Good to know
• The information in AyuBase6 is general guidance, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your exercise, diet or medicines.
• The app shows ads, after you agree to them.
